# Sample assessments

Three completed site assessments for a fictional company, Meridian, plus a portfolio rollup. They were run with OT Cybersecurity Assessment Tool v1.1 (question bank v2.1.0) in Detailed mode. The organizations, sites and findings are fictional.

| Sample | Site | Standard | Overall | Level | Risks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Riverside Production Facility (Chemical) | IEC 62443 | 1.9 | Initial | 20 |
| 2 | Bayport Distribution Terminal (Chemical) | NIST SP 800-82r3 | 2.9 | Managed | 20 |
| 3 | Lake Charles Pipeline Station (Oil & Gas) | TSA Pipeline Security Directives | 2.9 | Managed | 11 |
| 4 | Portfolio rollup of all three | Mixed | 2.6 | — | 51 |

## Files

- **Sample1–3 …_Report.doc**: each site's report exactly as the tool exports it. Word may warn that the format and extension don't match; click Yes to open it.
- **Sample4_Meridian_Portfolio_Rollup_Report.doc**: the multi-site portfolio report.
- **…_Report.pdf**: the same four reports as PDFs.
- **Sample1_Riverside_IEC62443_Raw_Data.csv**: the Export Raw Data (.csv) output for Riverside.
- **Assessment_Samples_Import_Me.json**: all three sites. In the tool, click **Import (.json)** and choose this file. Then tick all three on the Dashboard and click **View Portfolio Rollup**.
- **Dashboard.png**, **Results_Screen.png**, **Charts_Tab.png**, **Portfolio_Screen.png**: screenshots.
